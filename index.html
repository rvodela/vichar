Built to keep your sleep private

The short version. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

No account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing tied to your identity.
No audio upload, ever. Recordings and their on-device transcription never leave your phone, at any subscription tier.
AI is opt-in and yours. If you add your own API key, only the text you choose to send goes to that provider — never audio.
Keys stay on-device. Your AI provider key is stored in the phone's secure keychain, never on our servers — we don't have servers that see it.
Not a medical device. Vichar does not diagnose, detect, or monitor any health condition.
Delete anytime. Remove a night, its audio, or the whole app's data whenever you like.

Before you start

Vichar is for curiosity and entertainment.

  • Transcripts are automatic and often wrong. Sleep talk is mumbled; treat every quote as approximate.
  • Dream readings are informed guesswork, not analysis of you. They are generated from the words alone.
  • Vichar is not a medical device and does not detect, diagnose, or monitor any sleep or health condition. If something about your sleep worries you, talk to a doctor — not an app.
  • AI can hallucinate — state things confidently that are simply not true. Read every AI reading with that in mind.
